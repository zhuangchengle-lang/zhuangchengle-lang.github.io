Gameplays introduction

apt apt apt apt apt apt

Identity V is an asymmetrical multiplayer horror game. Each match usually involves five players: one player takes the role of the Hunter, while the other four play as Survivors. Both sides have different objectives and must use strategies and skills to win the game.


Besides this, there are many other ways to play.

some other gameplay

Duo hunters

Two Hunters compete against eight Survivors. Survivors must decode seven cipher machines, while Hunters work together to capture them. Players can also purchase items from telephone booths.

Black Jack

Players draw playing cards to collect points. The goal is to get as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it. Strategy is essential to avoid going bust.

Tarot

Two teams each have one King, two Squires, and one Knight. Squires protect their King, while the Knight tries to eliminate the opposing King
