RANKED MATCH

apt apt apt

Ranked Match is a competitive game mode in Identity V. Players can improve their ranks by participating in ranked matches. There are two factions: Survivors and Hunters. Both sides need to use their skills and strategies to win. Generally, a higher rank indicates more gaming experience and stronger competitive abilities.

Ranking Rules

The ranked system in Identity V consists of several tiers, from the lowest to the highest. Players need to earn stars through matches to gradually improve their ranks. Survivors and Hunters have different rank names, but their rank levels correspond to each other.
