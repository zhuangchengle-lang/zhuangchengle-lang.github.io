Survivor Rules

DECODE · RESCUE · ESCAPE

  • Work together to decode 5 cipher machines and build decoding progress.
  • Avoid the hunter and use the terrain to kite.
  • Rescue injured teammates from the rocket chair.
  • Open the gate and escape the manor to win.

Representative Survivors

Composer

Controls the scene with music and decodes ciphers quickly.

Lawyer

Can see the hunter's location on the map and guides teammates.

Perfumer

Uses perfume to rewind time and avoid damage.
